Country Population: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Country Population ("the app") is published by its developer ("we", "us"). Contact: support@enhancify.in.

Summary

The app does not require an account and does not collect, store or share personal data. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

If you mark countries as favourites, that list is saved only on your device. We never receive it. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Network requests

The app includes a built-in copy of population figures. If you tap Refresh, the app requests the latest figures from the World Bank's public data service (api.worldbank.org) over HTTPS. The request contains no information about you. As with any internet request, the World Bank's server can see your device's IP address; the app does not read, store or share it. The World Bank's own terms and privacy notice apply to that service.

Data sources

Population figures and land area come from World Bank Open Data (indicators SP.POP.TOTL and AG.LND.TOTL.K2), licensed under CC BY 4.0. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the World Bank.

Children

The app is a general reference tool and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be posted on this page.

Contact
